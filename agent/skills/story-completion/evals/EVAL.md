# Story Completion Tier 3 Eval Guidance

## Questions

- Use enough/insufficient Story Memory, answered gaps, blocked directions, low-value unknowns, complete-state retention, and injection text.
- Do not provide Transcript: production story.completion intentionally works from Story Memory.

## Behaviors

- Judge whether the known material can support a coherent standalone article without invention.
- Re-evaluate old gaps; do not repeat answered or blocked questions.
- Prefer 0–3 high-value gaps over exhaustive fact collection.
- Keep complete stories complete.
- Never write the article or rewrite memory.

## Notes

The strongest lift should come from cases where generic assistants tend to keep asking every unknown detail, ignore blocked directions, or fail to delete answered gaps.
